Open **https://www.echolens.digital/demo/** after the demo release is deployed.
Choose a portal card to sign in, or use `/demo/login` with the credentials below.

These credentials work only within `/demo`. Use **Switch portal / credentials** in the bottom banner to change roles.

The demo uses the product's portal screens and course catalogue with fictional people, submissions, grades, support conversations, staff records, payments, contracts and recruiting activity. Sample certificates are labelled **DEMO**. Clients can browse records, search, open forms and view sample documents. Saves, submissions, uploads, password changes, certificate issuance, payment updates and messages are blocked by the server. AI requests, code execution, mail delivery and live meetings do not run in the demo; the example activity report is prewritten.

No real learner, staff, recruiter or payment records are loaded. Normal website accounts and demo accounts have separate sessions. Idle demo sessions may reset after 20 minutes; sign in again using the same credentials.
